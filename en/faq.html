FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What families ask us most.

What ages is it for?

Ages 2 to 6. A grown-up picks the age group (Toddlers, age 2; Preschoolers, 3; Big kids, 4 to 6) and the activities adapt. The app never changes the group on its own.

Does my child need to read?

No. Tuni explains every game out loud. If it helps, you can turn on Tuni’s subtitles in the grown-up area.

What languages is it in?

American English, Spanish (with voices from Spain or Latin America) and Catalan. You choose when you start and can change it in the grown-up area.

Does it have ads?

No. Mundo Tuni has no ads and doesn’t ask you to sign up.

Does it work offline?

Yes, the games work offline. Buying and restoring the purchase need the internet. When there’s a connection, the app also checks the purchased access and, on iPhone and iPad, uses Apple’s standard campaign measurement, as the privacy policy explains.

What’s free?

Every world has free content: numbers 1 to 3, letters A to E, four colors, three shapes, one word and one rhythm. With Lola, four colors; in Math, quantities up to 3. The rest unlocks with a one-time purchase.

Is it a subscription?

No. It’s a single payment, with no subscription or recurring charges.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad.

Can I limit play time?

Yes. In the grown-up area you can choose 5, 10 or 15 minutes of active play. When time is up, Tuni ends with a rest screen. There’s no limit by default.

What data does it collect?

The app doesn’t ask for your child’s name, date of birth or an account for them. Progress and the age group stay on your device. The privacy policy has all the details.

What is the microphone for?

Only if a grown-up turns it on: in Lola’s corner, your child hears their own voice when repeating a word. The recording is kept briefly on the device, played back and deleted; if the app closes halfway, it’s deleted the next time it opens. It never leaves the device.

How do I get my purchase on another device?

In the grown-up area, tap “Restore Purchases” using the same Apple Account you bought with.

Do you recommend screens for my 2-year-old?

Not as a first choice: Spain’s pediatric association advises avoiding screens from 0 to 6, and the World Health Organization advises no more than one hour a day for ages 2 to 4, and less is better. If your family does use screens, guidelines advise watching together, choosing calm content and not using them to soothe. Mundo Tuni doesn’t replace playing with a grown-up.

Why are there no streaks or countdowns?

The American Academy of Pediatrics explains that many platforms are designed to keep kids engaged, with autoplay, frequent rewards or ads. Mundo Tuni has no ads, streaks or countdowns: kids earn a star for each item they complete (and, in the six main worlds, a sticker), and a grown-up can set a play time with a calm ending.
