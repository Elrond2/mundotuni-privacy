Support

Help for parents and caregivers using Mundo Tuni.

Need help? Write to us and we’ll reply within 2–5 business days.

hola@mundotuni.com

What ages is the app for?

Kids ages 2 to 6, with a grown-up nearby. A grown-up picks the age group (Toddlers, 2; Preschoolers, 3; Big kids, 4 to 6) and the app never changes it on its own. There are eight worlds: numbers, letters, shapes, colors, words, music, math (ages 4 to 6) and Spanish with Lola.

How much does the full content cost?

Every world has free content. The rest unlocks with a one-time purchase, with no subscription and no recurring charges; the store shows the price in your country.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the grown-up area (the Grown-ups button), solve the math challenge and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. If it doesn’t work, write to us without including your child’s details or any passwords.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. All activities work offline, including in airplane mode. Buying or restoring a purchase needs a connection, and when there is one the app checks the purchase status as it opens.

Do you collect data about my child?

We don’t ask for your child’s name, date of birth or an account for them. At the start, the app asks the grown-up for an approximate age (you can skip it) and saves only an age group on the device to adjust the difficulty; the app doesn’t send it to any service. Progress also stays on the device. For purchases, RevenueCat uses a pseudonymous technical identifier. All the details are in the privacy policy.

How do I erase progress?

In Grown-ups you can reset progress and stickers separately. If you uninstall the app, its local storage is erased, except for whatever system backups keep.

How do I ask you to delete the purchase profile?

In Grown-ups, tap Request deletion of the purchase profile. The app prepares an email with the technical RevenueCat identifier, and you decide whether to send it. Deletion doesn’t cancel or refund the purchase: you can recover it afterwards with “Restore Purchases”.

Help us improve Mundo Tuni

Your experience helps us, whether you bought or not. Tell us what your family finds most useful, where you got stuck or what you would change. We don’t ask for a specific rating and we don’t give rewards for feedback.

Please don’t include your child’s name, exact age, photos or any other personal details.
