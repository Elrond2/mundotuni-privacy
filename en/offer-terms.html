“Press and creators 2026” offer

What the code gives you

One code unlocks Mundo Tuni Pro (shown in the app as “Mundo Tuni Complete”), the one-time purchase that opens all the content of the app (it is not a subscription), at no cost. Each code works once, for one Apple Account.

Where and until when

  • Valid on the App Store in the United States, Spain, Mexico, the United Kingdom, Canada and Australia.
  • Redeem it before April 15, 2027. After that date it no longer works.
  • You need an Apple Account and to accept the App Store terms. Redeem it in the App Store app (your profile → Redeem Gift Card or Code) or with the link you received.

Good to know

  • The code has no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money and may not be sold or transferred for anything in return.
  • Receiving a code does not oblige you to write, post or rate anything. If you mention Mundo Tuni, please say clearly that you received free access.
  • Lost, expired or already used codes cannot be replaced, except when the code itself fails.

Who offers it

The offer and the app content are provided by the developer of Mundo Tuni (the developer named on the App Store page). Apple is not the sponsor of this offer. Questions: hola@mundotuni.com.

Privacy: Mundo Tuni privacy policy. The app doesn’t ask for your child’s name, date of birth or an account for them.
