In one sentence

Mundo Tuni is a learning games app for kids ages 2 to 6, in English, Spanish and Catalan, guided by the voice of Tuni, a baby tuna, with no ads and no internet needed.

In one paragraph

In Mundo Tuni, kids ages 2 to 6 play with numbers, letters, shapes, colors, words and music alongside Tuni, a baby tuna who explains everything out loud, so no reading is needed. Big kids also get a math world, and in the corner of Lola, a turtle, they learn words in Spanish (or in English, if they play in Spanish or Catalan). The app has no ads, needs no sign-up, works offline and keeps progress on the device. A grown-up area shows what they've practiced and lets you set a play time that ends calmly.
