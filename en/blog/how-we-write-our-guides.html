How we write our guides

What the Mundo Tuni byline means and how we distinguish sources, our own ideas and app information.

By Mundo Tuni. This is the editorial standard for our family guides. The byline identifies the brand behind the app and these articles; it does not claim a medical or teaching qualification or an independent professional review.

What these guides are for

They offer play ideas and general explanations for adults with children ages 2–6. An activity is an invitation you can adapt or skip. It is not a test of what your child should know or a promise of results.

What we distinguish in an article

  • Information from a source: it should include a link so readers can check what it says and whom it applies to.
  • Mundo Tuni ideas: these are our own play suggestions. Citing a general source does not mean that organization has tested or endorsed our games.
  • Information about the app: it should describe an available version. A prepared update must not be described as already released.

Sources and dates

Our standard is to consult the original document or responsible organization and link to it in the article. An access date tells you when a source was read, not when it was published or that it remains unchanged. An article’s update date should correspond to a specific review of its content.

If we cannot verify a claim, we should say so or remove it. Any health guidance cited needs its context: age, date and scope. These guides do not diagnose or replace professional advice.

Authorship and AI tools

Mundo Tuni uses artificial intelligence tools to help prepare text and translations. This support does not amount to human or expert review. We do not attribute these articles to a specialist or claim a person reviewed them unless that is documented.

Our relationship with the app

These guides belong to Mundo Tuni, which also publishes the app. App mentions describe our own product. You can try the screen-free games without downloading it. Read our commitments or visit the app introduction.

Corrections

If you spot a questionable detail, you can use the contact on our support page. The page address and passage are enough; you do not need to share information about your child. Our standard for corrections is to update the text and date when the content changes, without describing a pending check as completed.
