Privacy Policy

Mundo Tuni: Aprende Jugando · Version 1.7 · October 25, 2026

This is an English translation of the privacy policy, which was originally written in Spanish (es-ES). The original Spanish text is available at Versión en español; there is also a Catalan translation (versió en català).

The essentials

  • We do not ask for the child's name, date of birth, location or an account.
  • At the start, the app asks the grown-up for the child's approximate age (this can be skipped) and stores only an age band (age 2, age 3 or ages 4 to 6) on the device to adjust which games appear and how difficult they are. The grown-up can change it in the grown-up area. The app does not send it to any service.
  • There is no advertising and no analytics on the child's behavior.
  • Progress, preferences and usage counters are stored only on the device.
  • Purchases are handled by RevenueCat with a pseudonymous technical identifier (a random code, with no name or email); automatic collection of device identifiers is turned off.
  • On iOS, RevenueCat uses Apple's standard Apple Ads attribution (AdServices), without IDFA or cross-app tracking.
  • From the grown-up area, you can prepare a request to delete the technical purchase profile.
  • The grown-up area and purchases are protected by a parental check.
  • Only if a grown-up turns it on, the language corner uses the microphone so the child can hear their own voice when repeating a word. The recording stays on the device, is deleted as soon as it plays and is never sent.

1 Who we are

Data controller: the owner of Mundo Tuni, whose identification details (full name, Spanish tax ID and address) are given in the legal notice. Contact email: hola@mundotuni.com.

App: Mundo Tuni: Aprende Jugando
Bundle ID: es.mundotuni.tuni
Platforms: iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play)
Languages: Spanish, Catalan and English. The grown-up area is shown in the same language as the game.

This policy explains what data is involved when the app is used and, in section 14, on Mundo Tuni’s official social media profiles. Mundo Tuni does not ask for the child's name, date of birth or an account. It only asks, optionally, for the child's approximate age in order to choose an age band (age 2, age 3 or ages 4 to 6), which adjusts the games and their difficulty and is stored on the device (section 4). 2 Scope and users

Mundo Tuni: Aprende Jugando is an educational app for girls and boys ages 2 to 6, intended to be used under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian ("guardian").

It is designed from the start to protect children's privacy: it does not require registration or ask for information that identifies the child. The only question about the child is their approximate age, which is stored as a band and does not leave the device. The game and the educational content work offline. The purchase, restore and attribution features described in this policy do connect to external services when a network is available.

3 Data we do not ask for

The app does not ask the child or the grown-up for any of the following:

  • First or last name.
  • Email address or phone number.
  • Precise location (GPS).
  • IDFA or GAID advertising identifiers.
  • Biometric or health data, or photos.
  • Device contacts.
  • Camera. The microphone is only used if a grown-up turns on “Repeat with the microphone”, and the recording never leaves the device (section 4).
  • Browsing history or use of other apps.
  • Payment data or banking information.

The only question about the child is their approximate age, which the app asks the grown-up during the initial introduction, which can be skipped and which the grown-up can change later in the grown-up area. Neither the exact age nor the date of birth is stored, only a band (age 2, age 3 or ages 4 to 6), and only on the device (section 4).

If you write to us for help, to send feedback or to exercise a right, we will process your email address and the content of your message in order to reply (section 10). The hola@mundotuni.com mailbox is hosted on Apple's iCloud Mail. The holder reads the emails and may use artificial intelligence assistants (currently Claude, from Anthropic) to read them and prepare replies; the assistants read the emails to draw conclusions and suggest replies, and the confidential content of the emails is not stored outside the mailbox; that provider processes the data under its own terms of service and may do so in the US. No reply is sent without the holder’s approval.

No accounts and no advertising. The educational content does not need a connection. Mundo Tuni has no server of its own for progress: it uses RevenueCat, Apple and Google for purchases and, on iOS, AdServices for Apple Ads attribution.

5 Third parties and external services

The Mundo Tuni app does not include advertising, social network features or analytics on the child's behavior. It does use RevenueCat for purchases and, on iOS, Apple Ads attribution through AdServices. Specifically:

  • No advertising: there are no ads of any kind.
  • No behavioral analytics: Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude and similar tools are not used to observe the child.
  • No cross-app tracking: IDFA and GAID are not used, and the RevenueCat configuration turns off automatic collection of device identifiers.
  • No social networks in the app: there are no share buttons and no sign-in with other platforms. Mundo Tuni’s official social media profiles are described in section 14.
  • No server of our own: the developer has no servers, databases or cloud services for game progress.

RevenueCat (purchase management)

Purchases are managed with the RevenueCat SDK (RevenueCat, Inc., United States), which acts as a data processor. The SDK starts when the app opens to check whether there is premium access and to load the purchase options. To do so, it uses a pseudonymous technical identifier: a random code generated by the SDK itself that does not contain anyone's name or email. RevenueCat may also receive the technical metadata it needs to provide the service, such as the app and operating system versions or the IP address of the connection. When you buy or restore a purchase, it also receives the transaction information provided by Apple or Google.

The app does not assign the grown-up's name or email, or any data about the child, to that profile, and it expressly turns off automatic collection of device identifiers. Nor does the app send RevenueCat a custom paywall impression event: the counter of times the purchase screen has been opened stays on the device.

RevenueCat stores data on servers in the United States. That transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, included in its data processing agreement. You can ask us for information about these safeguards, and for a copy, at hola@mundotuni.com. More information in RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Apple Ads attribution (iOS only)

On iOS, when the app opens, the RevenueCat SDK obtains the attribution token that Apple provides through AdServices and sends it to RevenueCat, which asks Apple whether the install came from an Apple Ads campaign. If it did, RevenueCat stores in the technical profile the campaign data returned by Apple (campaign, ad group, keyword and attribution type). We use it only to measure, in aggregate, which campaigns work. This is Apple's standard attribution: it does not use IDFA, does not require the tracking permission (ATT), does not allow anyone to be tracked across apps or websites and does not include the child's name, age band or progress. It happens automatically, with no action by the grown-up or the child and before the parental check: the child does not see or choose anything in this process.

Request to delete the technical purchase profile

From the grown-up area, you can prepare an email to hola@mundotuni.com asking for the RevenueCat technical profile to be deleted. Only when the grown-up confirms does the app obtain the technical identifier and include it in the draft. The app does not copy that identifier into logs or into its own storage, and it does not send the email on its own: the grown-up sends it if they wish.

Deleting that profile does not delete local progress and does not cancel, refund or remove the purchase made with Apple or Google. Afterwards, you may need to use "Restore Purchases" to recover premium access.

App store ratings

When you enter the grown-up area, the app may ask the system to show Apple's or Google's rating dialog, and from that area you can also open the store page to write a review. The rating is handled by the store: the app does not receive the score or any account data.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). GitHub logs the IP address of visitors for security purposes, as described in its privacy statement. The website does not use cookies, and its fonts and images are served from the website itself.

Visit measurement. To know how many people visit the website and which pages interest them, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc., United States), which acts as a processor. When a page loads, the browser downloads a small Cloudflare script and sends it technical data about the visit: the page viewed, the website the visitor came from, the device type, the browser and the operating system; Cloudflare also infers the country from the connection. According to Cloudflare, this measurement does not use cookies or local storage, does not fingerprint the browser and does not log URL query strings. We only see aggregated statistics that do not identify anyone. According to Cloudflare, full visit-level data is kept for 7 days; after that only a sample (about 10%) is kept, and the dashboard shows statistics for the previous six months. The app does not include this measurement.

App Store links. Links from this website to the App Store carry a campaign code (for example, ct=web-portada) so that Apple can show us, in aggregate, how many product page views and downloads come from each page or channel. Apple does not tell us who downloads the app.

App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google may collect download and app performance statistics under their own privacy policies, over which we have no control. These operations are independent of how the app works.

6 AI-generated voice, sound and illustrations

Tuni's voice lines, the sound effects and the background music in the app were generated with artificial intelligence tools, as were the illustrations.

Tools and licenses. Tuni's voice, the sound effects and the music were generated with ElevenLabs (speech synthesis, ElevenLabs Sound Effects and ElevenLabs Music), under the commercial license of the Starter plan. The illustrations (logo, icons, backgrounds, stickers, creatures and word pictures) were created with ChatGPT. Full credits are on the "Credits" screen in the grown-up area.

All of this content was generated during development and ships inside the app. The app does not record the image of the user or the child. It only records the child's voice if a grown-up turns on “Repeat with the microphone” (section 4), and in that case it plays it back and deletes it on the device itself, without transcribing it. The app does not send data to ElevenLabs or OpenAI.

7 Parental control

The grown-up area and access to purchases are protected by a parental check: a simple arithmetic problem (an addition; for the 4 to 6 age band, a multiplication) that makes it harder for a young child to get into the settings, purchases and links that lead out of the app (this policy, support and email). Even so, we recommend that a grown-up supervise the use of the app.

The check:

  • Is solved entirely on the device, offline.
  • Does not keep any log of access.
  • Does not ask for a password, PIN or any personal data.

8 Purchases in the app

Part of the content is free, and the rest is unlocked inside the app.

Purchases are made only through the official payment systems of Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play). Payment is processed by the store: the developer does not receive card data or banking information.

To check, validate and restore premium access, the app uses RevenueCat (section 5), with a pseudonymous technical identifier and, when there is a transaction, with the purchase information provided by the store.

The purchase screen is always behind the parental check, as the store rules for children's apps require.

Mundo Tuni does not ask for the child's name, date of birth, email or an account. It only asks the grown-up, optionally, for the child's approximate age, which is stored as a band on the device to adjust the games and their difficulty. Progress and that band stay on the device. RevenueCat starts in order to check premium access and the purchase options with a pseudonymous technical identifier; buying requires an action by a grown-up after the parental check. On iOS, AdServices provides the Apple Ads attribution described in section 5.

  • The app does not ask for the child's name, date of birth or email.
  • The age band (age 2, age 3 or ages 4 to 6) is stored only on the device, and the app does not send it to any service.

10 Children in the European Union (GDPR)

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679) gives children's data enhanced protection.

What we process and on what legal basis

  • Data that stays on the device (progress, age band, preferences, counters and the rest of the data in section 4): we do not receive it. The app uses it on the device itself in order to work.
  • Purchases and restoring them: performance of the purchase contract requested by the grown-up (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Checking for a purchase when the app opens, having the purchase offer ready and measuring Apple Ads campaigns on iOS: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in keeping purchased access available without asking the grown-up for anything, in being able to offer the purchase and in knowing which campaigns work, using pseudonymous data, without IDFA and without the child's name, age band or progress. You can object by writing to hola@mundotuni.com.
  • Handling requests to exercise rights: compliance with a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Answering general questions and feedback: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in replying to people who write to us. When the handling is necessary to perform a contract with you or to take steps you requested before a contract, Art. 6(1)(b).
  • Measuring visits to this website (section 5): legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in knowing, through aggregated statistics and without cookies, how many people visit it and which pages interest them. You can object by writing to us or by blocking the static.cloudflareinsights.com script in your browser.

Without a connection, or without an Apple or Google account, you cannot buy or restore the purchase; the rest of the app works the same.

How long it is kept

Local data, until you uninstall the app; progress, stickers and counters, also until you reset them from the grown-up area (section 4). The RevenueCat technical profile, for as long as the app offers purchases and the profile is needed to restore them, or until you request its deletion; after an erasure request we will delete what is appropriate, except what we must keep because of a legal obligation (section 5). Support emails and rights requests, for as long as needed to handle them and, for rights requests, to be able to show that they were handled. Website visit statistics, under the Cloudflare periods described in section 5: 7 days with full detail and, after that, a sample that can be consulted for six months.

Privacy by design

  • Data minimization: progress and the age band stay on the device; RevenueCat has automatic identifier collection turned off, and the app reads the technical identifier only when the grown-up prepares a deletion request.
  • Purpose limitation: local data is used only for the purpose for which it is stored.
  • Confidentiality: the only external flows of the app are the purchase and attribution flows described in section 5; the social media profiles are covered separately, in section 14.

International transfers

The data processed by RevenueCat is stored in the United States, with the safeguards described in section 5 (Standard Contractual Clauses). Website visit measurement data is processed by Cloudflare, which may do so in the United States: Cloudflare participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and its data processing agreement provides for Standard Contractual Clauses if that framework stops applying. In addition, the website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States), which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes under its privacy statement (section 5). Apart from these cases, Mundo Tuni makes no international transfers of personal data on its own, except those described in section 3 (email and AI assistants) and section 14 (social media profiles).

Data Protection Officer

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer. For any question about data protection, write to us at hola@mundotuni.com.

11 Rights of parents and guardians

You can exercise these rights by writing to hola@mundotuni.com. To locate the technical purchase profile, use the request prepared from the grown-up area (section 5).

  • Access: find out what information is processed in connection with the use of the app.
  • Erasure: request deletion of the RevenueCat technical profile. Uninstalling the app only deletes local storage and does not replace this request.
  • Rectification: correct any inaccurate data.
  • Objection and restriction: object to processing based on legitimate interest or ask for it to be restricted.
  • Portability: receive the data in a structured format.
  • Complaint: to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es) or to the supervisory authority of your EU country.

We respond within one month at most from receiving the request.

  • The grown-up area is protected by the parental check described in section 7.

The security of the device (passcode, system updates, etc.) depends on whoever manages it.

14 Social media profiles

Mundo Tuni has official profiles on YouTube (@mundotuniapp), Instagram (@mundotuni) and TikTok (@mundotuni). This section explains how we handle data when we manage those profiles, their comments, their messages and their statistics. The app has no social network features (sections 3 and 5).

Controller

For the processing we carry out ourselves (reading and replying to comments and messages, moderating, reviewing statistics and handling requests to exercise rights), the controller is the holder of Mundo Tuni, whose identification details (name, tax ID and address) appear in the legal notice. Email: hola@mundotuni.com.

Each platform also processes data as the controller of its own services (the account, security, recommendations and advertising). Their privacy policies: YouTube (Google Ireland Limited), Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited) and TikTok (TikTok Technology Limited, Ireland, and TikTok Information Technologies UK Limited).

Who these profiles are for

The profiles are aimed at adults, in particular mothers, fathers and guardians. If we notice that a minor is writing to us, we do not hold commercial conversations with them or ask for further information, and we remove their personal data from the spaces we manage. This rule does not prevent us from giving the immediate attention needed or from reporting signs of violence to the authorities. We do not identify anyone by asking for documents, and we do not follow, tag or write to minors’ profiles.

What data we process and where it comes from

The data reaches us from you, through the platform, when you comment or write to us: your public name or username, your photo, the content of the comment or message and the date; it can also come from someone else who mentions you in a comment; if we receive data about you from someone else, we will give you the information required within the legal deadline, unless a legal exception applies. We also look at aggregated profile statistics provided by the platform (for example reach, countries and age ranges), which do not let us identify anyone.

We do not ask for special categories of data (health, for example) or data about minors, and we ask you not to include them in comments or messages. If we receive them, we avoid reproducing them, remove them from our public spaces, delete the copies under our control without delay and do not enter them into artificial intelligence tools, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • Replying to your comments and general enquiries, public or private: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact the brand (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). When the handling is necessary to perform a contract with you or to take steps you requested before a contract, Art. 6(1)(b). Requests to exercise rights are handled under a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Moderating the spaces we manage (hiding or deleting comments, blocking accounts): our legitimate interest in keeping them safe for families (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Reviewing statistics to assess how far posts reach and to adapt content for families: our legitimate interest in improving how we communicate about the app (Art. 6(1)(f)), using aggregated data.
  • Reporting to the authorities signs of violence against a minor: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c); Spanish Organic Law 8/2021, Arts. 15 and 19).

For processing based on legitimate interest, we have weighed that responding to people who write to us and moderating a space for families is compatible with your rights, because we use little data, we do not use it to make decisions based solely on automated processing with legal or similar effects, and you can object.

Following our profiles or writing to us does not mean you agree to receive private promotional messages: we do not use your enquiries to send them without a prior request or authorization, except where a legal exception applies. Any commercial communication will be identified as such and you will be able to object easily and free of charge. Contacting us is voluntary; without the essential information we may not be able to resolve your enquiry.

Mundo Tuni does not sell the data from interactions or use it to build personalized advertising audiences, and does not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects on you or significantly affect you. Each platform’s own processing, including its automatic moderation and its advertising, is described in its policies.

Profile statistics

The platforms give us aggregated statistics, with which we cannot identify anyone or access users’ personal data. For that specific operation (creating that data and aggregating it into statistics) the situation is as follows:

  • TikTok: TikTok and we are joint controllers of the processing (Art. 26 GDPR), under the TikTok Analytics Joint Controller Addendum and its information on TikTok Analytics. TikTok informs users about that processing and handles their rights requests; we are responsible for our legal basis (legitimate interest) and for the information in this section. To produce TikTok Analytics, TikTok collects interaction data about our profile and its posts (views, clicks and other actions, with the time, technical and demographic information described in its information on Analytics); we receive only the aggregated reports. Any other processing is carried out by each party as an independent controller.
  • Instagram: Meta publishes a joint controllership agreement for the statistics of its Facebook Pages (Meta’s addendum); its text does not specify whether it extends to Instagram professional accounts. For Instagram statistics, Meta Platforms Ireland Limited may act as a joint controller with us; we have not confirmed that this addendum applies to this Instagram profile; until we do, we treat the split as undetermined and handle any request from you about these statistics. Any other processing is carried out by each party as an independent controller.
  • YouTube: Google does not publish a joint controllership agreement for channel statistics and treats them as its own controller; we only receive the aggregated data.

Who receives the data

The relevant platform; for public comments, anyone who can see them; providers involved in the management (the hola@mundotuni.com mailbox is hosted on iCloud, section 3); and the competent authorities where there is a legal obligation: if there are signs of violence against a minor we promptly pass the necessary information to the competent authority and, if an offence may have been committed, to the police, the Public Prosecutor’s Office or the courts. There is no other disclosure.

Artificial intelligence. For comments and messages on the social networks, before using an AI assistant to prepare a reply, a person writes a general query that removes identifiers, distinctive details and traceable quotes.
